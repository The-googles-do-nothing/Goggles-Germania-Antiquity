GERMANIA ABILITIES SUMMARY
==========================

COMMANDER ABILITIES - KUNINGAZ (Unique Army Commander)
-------------------------------------------------------
The Kuningaz is Germania's unique Army Commander with the following promotion abilities:

1. ROUT (Level 1)
   - +3 Combat Strength for Infantry Units within the Command Radius when attacking

2. INCANTATIONS (Level 2)
   - +1 Happiness for each Unit packed in this Commander

3. FROZEN WARFARE (Level 3)
   - +3 Combat Strength for Infantry Units in Tundra Terrain in the Command Radius

4. VAE VICTIS (Level 4)
   - Defeating an enemy Unit provides Gold equal to 50% of its Combat Strength

5. HAFTAZ COMMENDATION (Unique Promotion)
   - Infantry Units in the Command Radius capture defeated land Units and place them under your control

6. POLEARMS COMMENDATION (Unique Promotion)
   - Bonus effects for Infantry Units


GREAT PEOPLE ABILITIES - THE FOLC
----------------------------------
Germany gains access to unique Great People (Folc) with distinct abilities:

MILITARY:
- Ariovistus of the Suebi: Grants an Army Commander
- Teutobod of the Cherusci: Activate on an Army Commander to grant 2 promotions
- Boiorix of the Cimbri: Grants 2 Units with increased Combat Strength

HAPPINESS & BUILDINGS:
- Vibilius of the Hermunduri: Library provides +4 Happiness in Settlement
- Raicunda of the Thuringii: Monument provides +4 Happiness in Settlement
- Veleda seeress of the Bructeri: Receive Amber Resource in Capital
- Pipara of the Marcomanni: +1 Happiness per Imported Resource in each Settlement

UNITS & MOVEMENT:
- Civilis of the Batavi: Grants a Settler Unit with increased movement
- Hermanafrid of the Thuringii: Grants 2 Units with increased movement


UNIQUE UNITS
------------

SUEBI (Unique Infantry Unit)
   - Deals Splash Damage
   - Tiered versions available (Tier 2, Tier 3)

BOAR WARRIOR (Unique Infantry Unit)
   - Movement restored when in vegetated terrain
   - Replaces Cavalry unit type
   - Tiered versions available

WOLF WARRIOR (Unique Scout Unit)
   - Can attack (unlike typical scouts)
   - Tiered versions available

KUNINGAZ (Unique Army Commander)
   - Ageless unit that persists across Age transitions
   - Unique promotion tree
   - Faster respawn than other commanders if defeated

SUBUI UNIT
   - Gained when constructing a Happiness Building in a Fishing/Farming specialized town


CIVIC ABILITIES
----------------

CODANUS
   - Gain a Naval Unit when you construct a Water Building

BARBARICUM (Multi-part)
   - +2 Happiness in Fishing/Farming towns for each City-State you are Suzerain of
   - +2 Resource Capacity to each town specialized as a Fishing/Farming town
   - Gain a Subui Unit when constructing a Happiness Building in a Fishing/Farming town

MIDGARD
   - +1 Codex slots in the City Hall when choosing a Fishing/Farming town focus

WULLFAZ (Multi-part)
   - +100% Commander experience during a Celebration
   - +50% Celebration duration
   - Grants special Commander "Hermann (Man of War)"


TRADITIONS
-----------

CODANNOVIA
   - +25% Gold towards purchasing Food or Happiness buildings

ALL MEN
   - +2 Happiness per Farm or Fishing Boat in all Fishing/Farming specialized towns

FOR LOVE OF COUNTRY (Associated with Pipara)
   - +1 Happiness per Imported Resource in each Settlement


WONDERS
-------

HASELGEBRIGE SALT MINE
   - +1 Influence to all Mined or Quarried Resources in the city
   - Ageless wonder
   - Must be placed adjacent to a Salt Resource


KEY THEMES
----------
- Emphasis on military strength and commander promotions
- Strong focus on Fishing and Farming town specialization
- Unique Great People (Folc) system with diverse abilities
- Unit abilities centered around terrain bonuses and splash damage
- Resource and happiness generation through specialized buildings
- Celebration mechanics enhancement
- Naval unit generation through water buildings
